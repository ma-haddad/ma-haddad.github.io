1. Nonlinear Optics in Natural and Bio-Based Materials

My research focuses on nonlinear optics, laser spectroscopy, and light–matter interactions in natural and bio-based materials. I investigate the nonlinear optical properties of edible oils and hybrid bio-nanomaterials under continuous-wave and pulsed laser excitation using techniques such as Spatial Self-Phase Modulation (SSPM) and Spatial Cross-Phase Modulation (SXPM). My work aims to understand the thermal and electronic mechanisms governing nonlinear refraction, reverse saturable absorption, and optical limiting.

A major direction of my research is the development of green photonic materials by combining natural media with two-dimensional nanostructures and nanosheets, to enhance nonlinear optical performance. These studies contribute to sustainable photonic technologies for applications in optical limiting, all-optical switching, laser protection, and advanced optical devices.
