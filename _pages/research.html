Research Interests

My research focuses on nonlinear optics, laser spectroscopy, and light–matter interactions in natural and bio-based materials. I investigate the nonlinear optical properties of edible oils and hybrid bio-nanomaterials under continuous-wave and pulsed laser excitation using techniques such as Spatial Self-Phase Modulation (SSPM) and Spatial Cross-Phase Modulation (SXPM).

A major direction of my research is the development of green photonic materials by combining natural media with two-dimensional nanostructures, particularly MoSe₂ nanosheets, for applications in optical limiting, all-optical switching, laser protection, and advanced photonic devices.
