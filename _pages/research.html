1. Nonlinear Optics in Natural and Bio-Based Materials

My research explores the nonlinear optical properties of natural and bio-based materials under continuous-wave and pulsed laser excitation. Using techniques such as Spatial Self-Phase Modulation (SSPM) and Spatial Cross-Phase Modulation (SXPM), I investigate thermal and electronic nonlinearities in edible oils, plant-derived compounds, and hybrid nanomaterials. My work focuses on optical limiting, nonlinear refraction, and light–matter interactions in eco-friendly photonic media. By incorporating two-dimensional nanostructures such as MoSe₂ nanosheets, I develop hybrid systems with enhanced nonlinear performance for applications in optical limiting, laser protection, photonic devices, and sustainable nonlinear optics.

2. Spectroscopy of Complex Materials

My research applies laser and synchrotron-based spectroscopy to investigate the structural, chemical, and optical properties of complex biological, mineral, and functional materials. I employ techniques including Raman, FTIR, LIBS, XRF, XRD, and X-ray absorption spectroscopy to characterize molecular composition, elemental distribution, and crystalline structure. These non-destructive methods support research in advanced materials, environmental science, heritage conservation, and biomedical applications, providing valuable insights into the composition and functionality of complex systems.

3. Synchrotron Applications in Medicine, Minerals, and Heritage Science

I use synchrotron radiation for high-resolution imaging and spectroscopy of biological, geological, and archaeological materials. My research employs techniques such as XRF, XAS, XANES, XRD, and synchrotron X-ray tomography to reveal elemental composition, chemical states, and internal microstructures without damaging valuable samples. Current projects include the characterization of biological tissues, mineral specimens, and historical artifacts from Yazd, Iran, contributing to advances in medicine, materials science, and cultural heritage conservation through non-destructive analysis.
