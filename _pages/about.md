<p style="text-align: justify;">I am an Assistant Professor of Physics at <a href="http://www.yazd.ac.ir/en" target="_blank">Yazd University</a>, affiliated with the Atomic and Molecular Physics group. My research activities are focused on optics, lasers, and spectroscopy, with particular interests in nonlinear optics, molecular physics, laboratory astrophysics, and laser-based applications in science and technology. My work aims to understand fundamental light&ndash;matter interactions and apply advanced optical techniques to the investigation and development of materials and photonic systems.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">I received my Ph.D. in Physics from&nbsp;<a href="http://www.vu.nl" target="_blank">Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (VU Amsterdam)</a> and <a href="https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/research/research-facilities/science/laboratory-for-astrophysics" target="_blank">Leiden University</a>, where I conducted research as a doctoral candidate and guest researcher. My doctoral dissertation, <em>&ldquo;Cavity Ring-Down Laser Spectroscopy of Carbon-Chain Molecules,&rdquo;</em> focused on high-resolution spectroscopic investigations of carbon-based molecular species and their relevance to astrophysical environments. Under the supervision of <a href="https://www.nat.vu.nl/~wimu/" target="_blank">Prof. Wim Ubachs</a>, <a href="https://www.iau.org/IAU/Shared_Content/Contacts/ContactLayouts/Obituary.aspx?ID=31574" target="_blank">Prof. Harold Linnartz</a>, and Dr. Dongfeng Zhao, my research contributed to connecting laboratory spectroscopy with astronomical observations, particularly in the study of diffuse interstellar bands.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">My current research combines laser spectroscopy, nonlinear optical phenomena, and advanced material characterization techniques to explore the optical properties of complex materials. I investigate nonlinear light&ndash;matter interactions in natural, biological, and hybrid materials, with applications in nonlinear photonics, optical sensing, laser protection, and sustainable photonic technologies. In addition, I apply spectroscopic and synchrotron-based methods for the structural and chemical characterization of advanced materials, biological systems, and heritage-related samples.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Through interdisciplinary research at the interface of physics, photonics, materials science, and spectroscopy, my goal is to develop a deeper understanding of optical phenomena and contribute to innovative solutions in emerging areas of laser science and technology.</p>
